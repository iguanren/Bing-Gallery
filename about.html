Why this site exists

为什么会出现这个网站

I got into building websites back in 2018, knowing nothing at first — reading tutorials while writing code as I went. I ran a forum, kept a blog, and stuck with both for quite a while. Later, work left me no time to maintain them, so I archived all the blog content and rebuilt the site.

For a long time I couldn't decide what it should be about — until I remembered that back in my blogging days, I loved setting Bing wallpapers as the page background. Bing releases one official HD wallpaper a day, then quietly swaps it out the next day, which makes it a pain to find again. So I decided to collect them and archive them properly, with the idea of browsing through them again on a quiet day. That's how this site came to be — Bing Gallery.

我从 2018 年开始接触建站，那时候什么都不懂，一边查教程一边敲代码，做过论坛，也写过博客，断断续续坚持了挺长一段时间。后来因为工作，没时间继续维护，就把博客内容全部做了存档，把网站重新改版。

最初我很纠结该做什么内容——直到想起做博客时，我总喜欢拿必应美图当网站背景。必应每天放出一张官方高清壁纸，第二天却悄悄换掉，想再找就很麻烦。于是我决定把这些数据收集起来，归档存好，闲暇时还能翻出来品鉴一番。就这样，有了这个网站——Bing Gallery。

What's on the site

这个网站有什么用

The homepage opens on today's wallpaper — China market first, then US — so what you see each day is always the latest.

Below it, a card flow shows the 30 most recent wallpapers; anything older is archived. Hit Archive to browse by month.

In the lightbox, arrow keys flip through images, and you can also tap the left or right side of the screen. See one you like? Download the full-resolution original straight to your device.

我把每天的新壁纸放在网站首屏，优先显示中区图片，其次是美区图片——所以你每天打开看到的，都是当天最新的壁纸数据。

首屏下方是一条卡片流，展示最近的 30 张壁纸。更早的图片做了归档，点 Archive 可以按月份浏览。

灯箱点开后，支持键盘左右翻页，也支持点击屏幕左/右区域翻页浏览。看到喜欢的图片，可以直接下载原图到你的设备。

Automatic updates

自动化数据更新

Wallpaper data is pulled daily from Bing's official API across 15 markets, updated three times a day by GitHub Actions. Every link is health-checked on each run; dead images are automatically replaced, so the page never goes blank.

History is kept in full and never deleted — in other words, every image you've seen here will stay here.

本站每日自动抓取必应官方接口，覆盖全球 15 个市场，由 GitHub Actions 每天自动抓取三次，同步做全量链接体检，失效的图会自动补位，不会出现页面走丢的情况。

历史数据全量留存、永不删除——换句话说，你在本站看到过的图片，都会一直在这里。
